The challenge

Large-diameter pipes are costly to transport.

For pipes at DN1500 and above, logistics can represent a disproportionate share of total project cost. Beyond cost, transport introduces schedule, pipe-integrity, and carbon risks.

Mobile Factory Solution

An on-site factory built for your project.

PolyGrande installs a complete, self-sufficient pipe production facility at or adjacent to your project site. It produces the pipes you need, when you need them, to the specification you require - with no transport chain.
